# CCT 5.6 candidate — target-evidence independence

Candidate 5.5 distinguishes target contexts semantically, but two apparently different targets can reuse the same samples, sampling frame, generator, adjudication chain, or failure domain. This candidate traces those roots and clusters shared support rather than counting labels.

Every target must disclose five material lineage roots, and target sample identifiers must be disjoint. The held-out confrontation passes 5.5 with two renamed contexts but is rejected because raw data, generator, and samples are shared.

This local synthetic candidate establishes only declared, testable separation of known lineage. Unknown lineage remains unknown; causal relevance, authorization, deployment, and external robustness are not established.
