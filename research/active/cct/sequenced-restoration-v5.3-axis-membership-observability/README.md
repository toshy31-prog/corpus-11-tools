# CCT 5.3 candidate — axis-membership observability

Candidate 5.2 trusts the axis labels attached to each observation. An intersection can therefore appear safe if harmed members are labelled out of it. This candidate requires complete pre-outcome classification of every observation on every open axis through at least two channels with distinct controllers and failure domains.

The channels must agree, and their resulting membership must exactly match the `debtAxes` used by the intersection analysis. A held-out confrontation passes 5.2 under the declared labels but is rejected when the independent measurements reveal concealed intersection members.

This remains a local candidate with synthetic fixtures. Agreement between channels does not by itself establish that the axis definitions are construct-valid, transportable, authorized, deployed, or externally robust.
