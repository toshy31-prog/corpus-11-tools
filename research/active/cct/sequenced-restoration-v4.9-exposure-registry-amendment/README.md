# CCT 4.9 candidate — exposure-registry amendment

The 4.8 registry closes an opportunistic-search channel, but permanent closure would prevent a genuinely missing exposure from ever being studied. This candidate defines a one-way amendment boundary: a proposal may become eligible only for a named future campaign and cannot change the current analysis.

Admission requires a committed operational definition, a falsifiable admissibility prediction, and at least two pre-outcome attestations with distinct source roots, controllers, and failure domains. The commitment digest makes later rewriting detectable. A proposal made after outcome access, supported by dependent attestations, or aimed at the current campaign is rejected.

This is a local research candidate. Passing its tests establishes neither construct validity nor causal relevance, future-campaign power, authorization, deployment, or external robustness. Effective admission still requires a later frozen campaign to show that the proposed variable adds a discriminating observable under adequate detection power.
