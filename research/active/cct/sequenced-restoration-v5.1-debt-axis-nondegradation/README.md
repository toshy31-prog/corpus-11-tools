# CCT 5.1 candidate — debt-axis nondegradation

Candidate 5.0 can admit a variable whose average held-out gain hides a loss concentrated on a debt-bearing group. This candidate requires the validation sample to cover every open debt axis and evaluates paired Brier improvement separately on each axis.

Each axis must meet a minimum sample size, exclude a materially negative lower confidence bound, and have enough precision to detect the declared axis-level difference. Thus an aggregate success cannot authorize admission when one open debt axis is degraded or unobserved.

This remains a local candidate with synthetic fixtures. Single-axis checks do not establish nondegradation at intersections, construct validity, causal relevance, authorization, deployment, or external robustness.
