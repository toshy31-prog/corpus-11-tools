# CCT 5.4 candidate — axis-construct calibration

Candidate 5.3 establishes observable, independently repeated labels, but two channels can faithfully reproduce the same invalid proxy. This candidate separates the debt construct, operational definition, observables, closest rival construct, discriminating observation, and reversal condition.

Every axis is calibrated against complete blind reference cases with independent adjudicators. Both positive and negative classes must be large enough, and Wilson lower bounds for sensitivity and specificity must exceed the frozen threshold. The held-out confrontation keeps both measurement channels in agreement while reference negatives expose proxy substitution.

The result is only `supported_for_scope`: local calibration does not establish causal relevance, transport to another population or protocol, authorization, deployment, or external robustness.
