# Defense Accountability Boundary

Do not choose between total disclosure and total opacity. Classify information by the harm of disclosure and the accountability function it serves.

## Workflow

1. Identify the protected operational detail and concrete disclosure risk.
2. Identify the non-sensitive facts required for authorization, oversight, recourse, and repair.
3. Separate public, protected-review, and secret channels.
4. Test whether each accountability claim remains independently auditable.
5. State gaps without exposing operational details.
