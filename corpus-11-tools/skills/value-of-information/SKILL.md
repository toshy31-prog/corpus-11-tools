---
name: value-of-information
description: Rank observations, experiments, searches, or measurements by their ability to change a live conclusion or decision relative to cost, delay, risk, and lost alternatives. Use for choosing the next test, stopping low-yield research, experimental portfolios, and deciding what evidence to gather.
---

Treat this skill as an invocation wrapper for the design candidate in `references/capability.md`; do not manufacture numerical expected value without probabilities, utilities, and a declared model.

1. Define the live decision, current conclusion, admissible alternatives, and deadline.
2. Read `references/capability.md` completely and load its critical dependencies.
3. For each candidate test, enumerate possible outcomes and which conclusion or action each could change.
4. Account for detectability, cost, delay, risk, opportunity loss, irreversibility, and dependence on other tests.
5. Report a qualitative dominance order by default; quantify only with explicit scales, probabilities, utilities, and sensitivity.
6. Stop or defer tests whose possible outcomes cannot change a material take.
