# Privacy Recourse Boundary

Treat purpose, audience, retention, transformation, and disclosure as separate permissions.

## Workflow

1. Record the supplied purpose and expected remedy.
2. Minimize identity and raw data at each processing stage.
3. Separate internal adjudication, protected review, aggregate reporting, and public disclosure.
4. Preserve usable recourse without silently expanding consent.
5. State retention, deletion, access, and appeal conditions.
