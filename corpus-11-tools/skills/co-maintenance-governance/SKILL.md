# Co-maintenance Governance

Do not infer authority from contribution or responsibility from access.

## Workflow

1. List roles and effective capacities.
2. Assign proposal, review, refusal, authorization, deployment, rollback, and repair separately.
3. Record vetoes, dependencies, escalation, and recourse.
4. Verify the active version and rollback owner.
5. State unowned duties and conflicting authorities.

Read `references/capability.md` for provenance and graph relations.
