name: scale-transition-assessment
description: Assess claims that a macro property, object, orientation, law, or collective behavior emerges from lower-level rules. Use for emergence, coarse-graining, micro-to-macro inference, aggregation, phase descriptions, injected order, and scale-dependent observables.
---

Treat this skill as an invocation wrapper for the design candidate in `references/capability.md`; a derivation under chosen aggregation does not by itself establish emergence.

1. Preserve the user's lower and upper levels and the claimed bridge.
2. Read `references/capability.md` completely and load its critical dependencies.
3. Compile every mapping, aggregation, boundary, symmetry choice, threshold, and external input in an information ledger.
4. Compare alternative coarse-grainings and lower-level controls capable of producing the same macro signal.
5. Report `derived_by_definition`, `injected`, `aggregation_dependent`, `emergence_candidate`, or `not_established`.
6. Never promote a macro interpretation beyond the observables that discriminate it.
