---
name: construct-validity-assessment
description: Assess whether an indicator, metric, score, operational definition, or proxy supports the construct claimed for it. Use when abstract phenomena are measured indirectly, labels may outrun observables, or proxy substitution and construct drift could alter a conclusion.
---

Treat this skill as an invocation wrapper for the design candidate in `references/capability.md`; defining a construct or metric does not validate their relation.

1. Preserve the user's construct and practical use.
2. Read `references/capability.md` completely and load its critical dependencies.
3. Separate construct, operational definition, measurement process, observed indicator, transformation, and interpretation.
4. Test content, convergent, discriminant, criterion, and intervention validity only where evidence exists; do not fill missing forms by analogy.
5. Report `supported_for_scope`, `partially_supported`, `proxy_substitution`, or `unknown`.
6. Name the closest alternative construct and the observation that would separate it.
