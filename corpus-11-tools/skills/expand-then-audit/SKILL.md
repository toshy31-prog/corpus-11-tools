# Expand Then Audit

Use only when several genuinely different mechanisms remain plausible.

First expand: generate independent candidates in the user's terms and preserve their differences. Then audit: test evidence, costs, attribution, protections, recourse, and reversal conditions. The audit may reject or qualify candidates but must not silently replace them with corpus-shaped alternatives. Stop when additional candidates no longer change the decision. Read `references/runtime.md` for provenance and limits.
