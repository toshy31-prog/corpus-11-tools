---
name: strategic-adaptation-assessment
description: Assess how people or systems adapt when a metric, test, rule, threshold, or evaluator becomes visible and consequential. Use for Goodhart effects, gaming, benchmark saturation, compliance theater, metric targets, adversarial adaptation, and burden shifting.
---

Treat this skill as an invocation wrapper for the design candidate in `references/capability.md`; identifying incentives does not predict a unique equilibrium.

1. Preserve the user's actors, stakes, rule, and intended outcome.
2. Read `references/capability.md` completely and load its critical dependencies.
3. Compare behavior before exposure, after observation, after learning, and after enforcement where evidence exists.
4. Map adaptation channels, detection channels, unequal costs, displaced harms, and second-order responses.
5. Report `stable_under_adaptation`, `adaptation_fragile`, `perverse_response_observed`, or `strategic_effect_unknown`.
6. Separate deliberate gaming from ordinary learning, constraint response, and measurement drift.
