# Terminal Recovery Assessment

Separate nominal controls from effective terminal and recovery capacities.

## Workflow

1. Define the failure threshold and who can observe it.
2. Identify who can stop the process and under which dependencies.
3. List options lost at stop time and who bears each loss.
4. Test terminal action, safe state, rollback, and recovery separately.
5. Record residual traces, reactivation channels, and remaining debt.

Call recovery tested only after a representative restoration succeeds. Read `references/capability.md` for provenance and graph relations.
