# Open Experiment Arena

Treat this as an experimental procedure, not a capability and not evidence by itself. Use the existing runner described in `references/contract.md` when executable comparison is requested.

1. FREEZE_SCENARIO — Freeze world dynamics, source regime, outcome dimensions, information channels, action space, exogenous sequence, stopping rule, and reversal conditions before inspecting contender outcomes.
2. RECORD_SOURCE — Mark the scenario `internal_synthetic`, `mixed`, or `external_supplied`. Never relabel internal authorship as external evidence. External scenarios require an independent authorship trace and pre-contender freeze.
3. REGISTER_CONTENDERS — Include at least two serious rivals. Give them matched initial worlds, exogenous events, information, time, and action budgets. Keep a no-specialized-framework baseline when it can discriminate the claim.
4. PREDICT_FIRST — Require each contender to state an admissible action and predictions before the world transition executes.
5. RUN_BLIND — Conceal contender identities in the public report. Keep the identity map separate. Prevent projections, observers, and narratives from mutating world truth.
6. PRESERVE_VECTOR — Report each outcome dimension separately. Never manufacture a scalar winner unless an independently authorized decision rule existed before the run.
7. COMPARE — Separate predictive accuracy, material consequences, institutional admissibility, hidden costs, and unknowns. Preserve equivalent survivors when the scene does not discriminate them.
8. CLASSIFY_STATUS — A successful internal run establishes only synthetic performance on the frozen scenario. It does not establish external validity, field usefulness, deployment, or robust capability.
9. MUTATE_OR_STOP — Retain, bound, revise, deactivate, or fork a contender only from a named discriminating result. Record anomalies that fit no current Corpus category without immediately compiling them into a new capability.

Forbidden:

- letting narrative text determine causal consequences;
- changing scenario dynamics after seeing contender behavior;
- comparing unmatched information or action budgets;
- calling a maintainer-authored fixture external;
- averaging incompatible outcome dimensions into a hidden verdict;
- promoting an arena success directly into the operational graph;
- forcing anomalies into the current Corpus vocabulary.
