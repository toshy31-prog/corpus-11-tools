# Temporal Power Assessment

Treat time as an allocated capacity, not neutral background.

## Workflow

1. Map who controls cadence, deadlines, queues, pauses, and expiration.
2. Measure losses per actor under the same delay or acceleration.
3. Check whether a pause preserves options or merely suspends visibility.
4. Identify traces, deliberation, or recourse destroyed by speed.
5. State the temporal mechanism, beneficiaries, loss bearers, and reversal condition.
